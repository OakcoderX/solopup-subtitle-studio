Caption the sounds that carry the story.

SDH review needs more than a transcript. SoloPup keeps ordinary subtitles, accessibility captions and review decisions together around the video.

A practical review loop

  1. Start a subtitles/SDH project and load the final video with the appropriate tracks.
  2. Replay each passage. Check who speaks and which sounds affect comprehension.
  3. Edit the SDH cue, its timing and its relationship to the dialogue.
  4. Save review decisions, then export the chosen track as SRT or ITT.

An illustrative editorial decision

Dialogue: “Who’s there?”

Potential SDH cue: “[knocking at the door]”

Invented example, not recognition output. Use a sound caption only if the final soundtrack supports it and it matters to the scene. Wording and timing need a human reviewer.

Evidence stays with the review

Loop a passage, adjust playback speed and inspect the subtitle frame grid. Preserve edits and review history. A successful export does not establish accessibility quality or a platform’s acceptance.
