SDH review: listen, decide, then caption.

Print this page or use it beside the final video. An editorial aid, not a platform standard.

Establish the source

  • Confirm the final video and audio mix.
  • Identify the ordinary and SDH tracks correctly.
  • Record the receiving profile and any title-specific exceptions.

Review every sound candidate

  • Is the event actually audible in this mix?
  • Does it affect understanding, action, mood or a character’s response?
  • Is the identity or source supported, or would naming it reveal information too soon?
  • Is the wording concise and specific without interpreting an unsupported emotion?
  • Does the timing follow the audible event while preserving readable dialogue?

Review the experience

  • Watch with sound, then read a passage without relying on it.
  • Check dialogue, music, effects and speaker cues together.
  • Record keep/revise/remove decisions and unresolved questions.
  • Retain review-draft status until the responsible reviewer approves.

In the real workspace, a sound candidate and its evidence can be inspected separately from final caption approval.
