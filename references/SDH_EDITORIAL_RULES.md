# Generic audio-grounded SDH policy

Use the final audio/video as evidence. Transcribe intelligible words accurately; never invent words, speaker identities or sounds from a screenplay or picture alone. Preserve original words while adjusting timing and segmentation.

Include audible non-speech information when it contributes to story, emotion or understanding. Prefer concise, neutral sound descriptions; avoid repeated labels for an unchanged music bed and incidental sounds that add no information. Consider what the picture and dialogue already convey. Return uncertain source/significance as a review question.

Choose formatting, punctuation, speed, timing, speaker identification, songs and music notation from the recipient's current language/title-specific requirements. Do not treat a single project's 120-WPM policy or a model's confidence as a universal platform rule. Lyrics, sentence continuations, complete dialogue sentences and bracketed sound labels require different punctuation treatment.

Keep source timing and export offsets separate. Preserve source evidence and unresolved disagreement; local rule checks never sign human or platform approval.
