# 日本語字幕 reference notes

[Reference index](README.md) · [Implementation status](implementation.md)

Japanese subtitles need their own reading-speed, character-count and segmentation rules. Horizontal and vertical text may have different line limits. Short, interrupted turns, multiple speakers, forced narratives, names and punctuation require Japanese-specific treatment; do not copy an English dual-speaker layout by default.

Current automatic project targets are English US/UK. This Japanese reference page does not enable or validate Japanese automatic production. Obtain the recipient's current Japanese guide, confirm exceptions and perform Japanese-language review before delivery.
