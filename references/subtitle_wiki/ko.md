# 한국어 subtitles reference notes

[Reference index](README.md) · [Implementation status](implementation.md)

Korean subtitles need language-specific weighted counts, grammar-aware line breaks, name/address consistency and honorific review. Hangul, Latin letters, digits, punctuation and spaces may carry different weights under a recipient's guide. An English CPS measurement is not a substitute for that definition.

Current automatic project targets are English US/UK. Korean reference availability does not establish verified Korean automatic production. Check the actual revision date inside the authoritative guide as well as its filename, and confirm current recipient requirements before delivery.
